Collaborations

Partnerships in metabolomics and exposomics.

The Huan Lab collaborates on biological, environmental, and health-focused projects where small-molecule measurement and data interpretation can create new insight.

TMIC and the Huan Lab

Dr. Tao Huan is the University of British Columbia node leader in The Metabolomics Innovation Centre (TMIC), Canada's national metabolomics core facility network. Through TMIC and the Huan Lab, collaborators can connect analytical chemistry, mass spectrometry, exposomics, bioinformatics, and data interpretation for biomedical, environmental, agricultural, and health-focused studies.

The Huan node focuses on mass spectrometry-based metabolomics and exposomics, with integrated analytical and computational workflows for large-scale small-molecule data. This includes method development, metabolomics data processing, metabolite annotation, disease biomarker discovery, mechanistic biology, and characterization of environmental exposures.

National network

TMIC node at UBC

TMIC brings together metabolomics nodes across Canadian universities. The UBC Huan node contributes expertise in analytical and bioanalytical chemistry, metabolomics, exposomics, and bioinformatics.

Exposomics

Complex biosample assays

TMIC describes the Huan node's exposomics assay as untargeted UHPLC-HRMS profiling of endogenous metabolites and chemical exposures in sample types such as teeth, bone, urine, hair surface, and hair strand.

Research interface

From samples to interpretation

Collaborative projects may involve study design, LC-MS data acquisition strategies, feature detection, metabolite annotation, statistical interpretation, and integration of metabolomics with biological or environmental questions.

Applications

Health and environment

TMIC highlights Dr. Huan's work in metabolomics and exposomics, including links among metabolism, gut microbiome, environmental factors, disease mechanisms, and maternal sugar overconsumption studies.
